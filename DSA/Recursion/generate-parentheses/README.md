## 📝 Problem Statement

Given an integer n.Generate all possible combinations of well-formed **parentheses** of length **2 x N** .

### Example 1

<p>

**Input:** n = 3</p><p>

**Output:** [ "((()))" , "(()())" , "(())()" , "()(())" , "()()()" ]</p>

### Example 2

<p>

**Input:** 2</p><p>

**Output:** [ "(())" , "()()" ]</p>

### Constraints

- 1 <= n <= 8

---

## 💡 Complexity Analysis

- **Time Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(N)$
- **Space Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(1)$
